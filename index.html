Everything you need to know about CogniLink AI.

CogniLink AI is an intelligent AI orchestration platform that automatically routes your requests to the best-suited AI model — whether that's for coding, image generation, writing, or live web research. Instead of manually switching between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or other tools, CogniLink handles it all from a single interface.
ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are individual AI models. CogniLink is an orchestration layer that sits above them — intelligently routing your task to whichever model excels at it, or running multiple models in parallel for comprehensive results. Think of CogniLink as the conductor, and ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini as the musicians.
OpenRouter is an API aggregator aimed at developers who already know which model they want to use. CogniLink is a fully intelligent orchestration platform — it automatically classifies your request, picks the right AI engine, runs them in parallel when needed, and delivers a unified response. No technical knowledge required.
